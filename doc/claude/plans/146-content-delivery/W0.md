# @PLN146 W0 — the corpus, its oracle, and the grammar arc W owes

The phase gate for **W0**, and the specification the rest of arc W is measured against.
The plan and its other phases are in [README.md](README.md).

## The rig

[`corpus/`](corpus/) holds three things and one command:

| | |
|---|---|
| [`corpus/scenes/`](corpus/scenes/) | every `.draw` scene that exists — 36 from `crawler/assets/sprites/src/`, plus loft's own `sketch/scene.draw` as `old_woman.draw` |
| [`corpus/oracle/draw.py`](corpus/oracle/) | the **existing Python renderer**, copied from `crawler/tools/draw.py`. Read-only here; crawler owns it |
| `corpus/golden/` | one committed PNG per scene, rendered by that oracle |
| `./corpus/w0.sh` | renders all 37 and compares; `--bless` rewrites the goldens |

`w0.sh` goes red three ways, and all three were provoked before the goldens were
blessed: a line no command accepts (`--once` exits 1 and names it), a failing
`check`, and a render that stops matching its golden. Today it is green, and green
twice in a row — the seeded `Fronds` field reproduces run to run, which is the
precondition W4's cross-backend gate needs.

## Where the corpus actually is

The plan said `crawler/tools/`. The scenes are in `crawler/assets/sprites/src/`;
`tools/` holds the renderer. A workspace-wide sweep for `*.draw` finds **37 files and
no others**, so the corpus is complete rather than merely plausible.

## loft's own `sketch/draw.py` is a stale fork

`crawler/tools/draw.py` (783 lines) is `sketch/draw.py` (584 lines) plus `--once`,
unparsed-line reporting, `Background transparent`, `Petals` and `Fronds`. Rendering
`old_woman.draw` through both produces **byte-identical** PNGs, so the crawler copy is
a strict superset on the shared grammar and is the only oracle arc W needs. Anyone
rendering a corpus scene with loft's copy gets a renderer that silently drops half the
marks; W2 replaces both, and `sketch/draw.py` should go when it does.

Point syntax: smooth points (`~`) in **15** scenes, per-point width (`@N`) in **21**.

point parser rather than in a later phase.

## Five things this changes in arc W

1. **`Petals` has no consumer.** W4 is cut as "`Petals` and `Fronds`" at M. `Fronds` is
   used by 8 scenes with its full non-uniform parameter set; `Petals` by none, anywhere
   in the workspace. Build `Fronds`; leave `Petals` until a scene asks for it. This is
   the shrink W0 was cut to find.
2. **`landmark` has no consumer either, and `check` has one scene.** W5's report channel
   is worth having for the reason the phase already gives — a metric report is cheaper to
   read than a PNG — but it is being built for the *next* author, not for the corpus. Cut
   it to `check` + `--once` and drop `landmark` unless W5's own scenes want it.
3. **`Background transparent` is the real background command**, used by 36 of 37 scenes,
   and it is not in `sketch/`'s documented grammar at all. The grayscale `top=`/`bottom=`
   form has **zero** users; the colour `topc=`/`botc=` form has one. A sprite renderer
   whose default background is anything but transparent is wrong for the whole corpus.
4. **`fill=` (grayscale) is unused; `grad=` is used once.** W3 is cut as "solid, linear
   gradient, radial gradient". Solid means `rgb=` — 361 lines. Radial is 19 Circles.
   Linear is **one** `Poly` in `old_woman.draw`. W3 stays S, but its weight is radial,
   and `fill=L` can wait with `Petals`.
5. **W1 undercounts the primitive gap — there are two, not one.** W1 is cut as "the one
   primitive genuinely missing (only `fill_triangle` exists today)". `graphics` ships 15
   `Canvas` drawing primitives and `imaging` ships PNG in and out, and **neither has any resample
   at all** — no `resize`, no downscale, nothing between a 768×768 buffer and a 256×256
   PNG. So W1 owes the polygon fill *and* a Lanczos downscale, and the second one is the
   harder to get pixel-identical because a filter is a numeric contract rather than a
   coverage rule. Both go in `graphics` beside `fill_triangle`; the phase stays S only if
   the downscale is written against Pillow's Lanczos rather than against a fresh one.

Two smaller ones: `Circle` never takes `n=` or a stroke in the corpus (it is always
filled or radial-filled), and `Line` never takes `stroke=` — the coloured-stroke texture
channel is entirely `Poly`'s. Neither is a reason to omit the option, but both say where
the pixel-comparison effort goes.

## What W2 must reproduce exactly

The oracle draws at **3×** and downscales with **Lanczos** (`draw.py:708`, `:736`), and
every golden here carries that. A loft port that draws at 1×, or resamples any other way,
cannot be pixel-identical whatever else it gets right — so the supersample factor and the
filter are part of the contract W2 signs, not implementation details it may choose. That
is what finding 5 turns into W1's second primitive.
